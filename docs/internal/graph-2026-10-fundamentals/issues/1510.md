## Problem

18 `@deprecated` JSDoc tags exist in non-test source; none names a removal in a parseable form (two mention a version loosely). Deprecation here is a label, not a lifecycle stage: nothing is ever scheduled out, which is how 6 commands, 64 of 89 flags and 26 of 65 settings reached zero use while still shipping (usage-audit.md). The rule is small: a `@deprecated` tag says `remove in vX.Y`. Default version for the 18 tags is the next minor after `package.json`'s current version; the owner may raise any of them on the PR. Nothing is removed here (F-10, D-2).
