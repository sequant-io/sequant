## Problem

The GTM brief's cold reader, a senior engineer who runs agents daily, named the objection the audience holds: "the agent grades its own work; who verifies the verifier." The product already answers half of it: the QA phase runs in a separate session, and the 2026-10 second-look study attributes 10 of 27 would-ship catches to that separation. The verdict comment does not say so. It reads `## QA Verdict: AC_MET …` with counts and gaps, and nothing tells a reader whether the author or a separate session wrote it, on which driver, on which model.

The data to say it already exists. The phase record carries `requestedModel` and `resolvedModel` (#971, #1198), the run log's phase entry carries `model` when the driver reports usage, and per-phase `agent`/`model` overrides exist in settings (#914, #1150), so a QA phase can already run on a different driver from the exec phase. That knob is the real independence step, and it is undocumented as such.
