### Why close rather than build Phase B

Not "no signal" — **signal too dilute to survive being read**. 3 true positives against 14 residual false flags is ~4.7:1. #608 measured the last two always-on LLM gap sections at a 0% action rate; a gate that cries wolf four times per real catch is how that outcome is reached. All three hallucinations are also cheap to catch downstream: `executeIssue` is contradicted the moment anyone opens `batch-executor.ts`.

And a path-only gate — the cheap, reliable half — would have caught **nothing**: 0 phantoms across ~700 path citations.

### Two limits on this number, stated plainly

- **It is an existence score, not a grounding-quality score.** It catches the loud failure and is blind to the quiet one: a real path described incorrectly. @zeweihan's first review point is untouched by this data. Nothing here says plans *understood* what they cited.
- **Coverage is 51.6%**, so about half of all claim lines carry no mechanically-checkable citation and were never in the denominator.

### What survives

Symbols are where all 3 true positives were and where the path half contributed nothing. I'd want a second measurement first, since the residual noise is concentrated in symbol extraction.
